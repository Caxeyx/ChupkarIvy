**What is ChupkarIvy?**
ChupkarIvy is a Discord music bot that acts like a Spotify speaker.

_What does that mean?_
Well think of it as being together with a group of friends, and playing some music over a bluetooth speaker.
That is what ChupkarIvy does, but instead of being a bluetooth speaker, it's a Discord bot!

**Do I need Spotify Premium?**
**_Yes_**, Spotify Premium is required for this bot to work.
This is a limitation set by Spotify, and even if this wasn't the case ChupkarIvy still wouldn't allow free users.
This is because ChupkarIvy does not support Spotify Free "features" (ads, limited skips, etc).
